You are compiling prose for an audience with no specialist background.

TARGET STYLE REQUIREMENTS:
- Tone: calm, patient, and plain.
- Register: everyday language.
- Audience: intelligent child or adult with no domain knowledge.

STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Preserve the order and logic of the argument.
- Do not remove steps, but make each step explicit using only content licensed by the authoritative source.
- Long sentences should be broken into shorter ones.
- Abstract concepts should be expressed in simple language without inventing explanatory content.

LANGUAGE CONSTRAINTS:
- Avoid jargon wherever possible.
- If a technical term must be used, explain it immediately in simple words when the authoritative source supplies enough information to do so faithfully.
- Prefer concrete phrasing over abstract nouns where meaning is preserved.
- Avoid metaphors that require prior knowledge or are not licensed by the authoritative source.

GESTURE CONSTRAINTS:
- Do not sound patronising.
- Do not introduce humour, characters, or stories unless already present.
- Do not simplify by removing important distinctions.

CITATION EXPECTATIONS:
- Scholarly citations are not required merely by this target.
- Preserve citations supplied by the authoritative source when they are part of the authored content.
- Do not demand or invent references as part of simplification.

FORMAT:
- No commentary about simplification.
- Do not add examples that are not already present in the authoritative source.
